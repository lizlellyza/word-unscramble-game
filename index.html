🎮 Word Unscramble

📖 How to Play

🎯 Objective

Unscramble the letters to find the correct word. Answer as many as you can!

🎮 How It Works

  1. Enter your name to join the game
  2. Choose a difficulty level: Easy, Moderate, or Difficult
  3. See scrambled words one by one
  4. Type the correct answer or use hints
  5. Skip words you don't know
  6. View the leaderboard to see all players' scores in real-time

⭐ Multiplayer Features

  • 👥 Live Leaderboard - See all players' scores
  • 🏆 First-to-Answer Tracking - See who solved each word first
  • 📊 Real-time Sync - Scores update instantly across all devices
  • 📧 Email Results - Send your results after playing

💡 Tips

  • Use the Hint button if you get stuck
  • You can Skip any word you don't know
  • Type your answer and press Enter or click Submit
  • Compete with your colleagues in real-time!
